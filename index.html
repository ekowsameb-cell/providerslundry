FAQ

Good questions

Can't find your answer? Ask our assistant in the corner or WhatsApp 020 277 5247.

How much laundry fits in one bag?

It depends on your plan: 8kg per bag on Basic, 10kg on Standard and 12kg on Premium. As a rough guide, 8kg is about a week's clothes for one person.

How long until I get my clothes back?

48 hours on Basic and Standard. Premium gets express 24-hour turnaround, and express is also available on one-off orders.

Is pickup and delivery really free?

Yes. Pickup and delivery are free anywhere in our Cape Coast service area, with no minimum.

Can I order without a monthly plan?

Yes. Use the online order form or WhatsApp us for a one-off order and we'll give you a quote.

How do I pay?

Our team will confirm payment options when they confirm your pickup. Just ask on WhatsApp if you have a preference.

Do you handle delicate fabrics?

Yes. Delicate and formal wear goes through our dry-cleaning service. Point out any special items when you book.
